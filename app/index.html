Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. It is long, not hard. Cheats switch off trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy save.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 h

    Play the story in order and do optional missions as they appear. Before you finish Chapter 1, do Lucia’s balcony meditation (see Missables) and keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go; the counts carry into 100%.

  2. Stage 2 — Side stories & Strangers & Freaks ~20 h

    Finish the five side-story strands (Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone) and eight Strangers & Freaks strands. Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s strands have their own trophies and do not count toward Strangers & Freaks — check the counter after each one.

  3. Stage 3 — Robberies & crime ~15 h

    Jewelry stores (Snap Gun from Chapter 3), armored trucks, shops with your partner, bank vaults (Chapter 4), and one illegal operation per gang. Vault escapes with your partner also cover Hot Together.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities & collectibles ~40 h

    Races, drone races, shooting ranges, fishing, arcades, meditation spots, card sets, WAiNK It locations, garages and vehicle registrations. Most of these are pleasant cleanup once the map is open.

  5. Stage 5 — The long grinds ~50 h

    250 stunts (300 for 100%), 50 Challenges (70 for 100%), 100 vehicles discovered (200 for 100%), and the Criminal Profile actions. These fill up fastest when you stop fast-travelling and drive everywhere.

  6. Stage 6 — 100% completion cleanup ~15 h

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the 35-task checklist down to zero. When it hits 100%, A Long Road pops — and with it, Vice Got a Price!

Missables — only one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot

Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward this trophy and disappears after that chapter ends. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time). She heads out to the balcony of Jason’s house to meditate — follow her and accept the optional task. It is not confirmed that this exact spot is required, so do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

Save strategy (no mission replay, no New Game Plus)

  • Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — rotate the rest.
  • Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race the moment it appears.
  • Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist.
  • Never use cheats on your trophy save — they should switch off both trophies and saving.

Sources & accuracy

The trophy list below is the list as reported at launch on 19 November 2026: 55 trophies (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze), 28 hidden, worth 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox, all single-player.

  • Exophase (via Push Square’s report of the PS5 list) — the source for the count and the Platinum.
  • Xbox achievement list — the source for names, descriptions and Gamerscore values; grades for PlayStation come from trophy hunters who have unlocked them.
  • OutputLag and GTA Vibes trophy guides — cross-checked for every name, grade and unlock method on 19 November 2026.
  • KeenGamer — roadmap and missable-trophy details.
  • PSNProfiles community — the earlier circulating list was picked apart there as probably fake; the list in this guide is the launch-day one, not that leak.

Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not published an official trophy list. A few hidden trophies still have no confirmed unlock method at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) — their descriptions are the official ones, and the guides for them say what is known so far. The Chapter 2 trophy name is printed censored by the reporting sources.
